About your Counsellor and Coach

  • I have always felt a deep calling to work with people. From my teenage years, I found myself drawn to understanding others, listening to their stories, their struggles, and helping them discover their strengths and potential. That early passion has grown into a meaningful career as both a Registered Counsellor and an NLP Life Coach, where I have the privilege of walking alongside individuals on their journeys toward growth and healing.
  • I am proudly registered with the HPCSA as a Registered Counsellor, and I have further expanded my skillset through my Life Coaching certification with Action Factory Global. For me, learning never stops. I actively pursue ongoing courses throughout the year and continuously engage in research to ensure that I am offering my clients the most relevant, effective, and compassionate support possible.
  • My passion lies in helping individuals from all walks of life, regardless of age, race, gender, cultural background, religious beliefs, or the challenges they face. I believe that every person has inherent strength and potential, even if it may not always feel that way. In our sessions, while we may begin by exploring the challenges that brought you in, my focus is always on how we move forward together. This is a collaborative journey — we are a team.
  • A big part of my work involves gently guiding my clients back to themselves by reminding them of their strengths, their resilience, and their capacity for growth. I place strong emphasis on purpose: understanding the purpose behind past decisions, as well as discovering the purpose in what lies ahead. When we begin to see our lives through this lens, even the most difficult experiences can become meaningful stepping stones.
  • Looking ahead, I am excited to expand my practice into Equine Assisted Psychotherapy (EAP). This is a space I am deeply passionate about, and I look forward to offering this unique and powerful therapeutic experience in the future.
  • At the heart of everything I do is a simple belief: you are capable, you are resilient, and you do not have to navigate life's challenges alone. Let us work together to define your purpose and make the most of your life. After all... You Only Live Once, so why not make it a life full of PURPOSE!

Mission Statement

My mission is to empower every person that steps into my room. I aim to be a listening ear and guide my clients to aim for the stars. I am here to remind them how capable they are to hop out of their comfort zone and into new opportunities.
